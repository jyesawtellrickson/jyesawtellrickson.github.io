Any sufficiently powerful AGI will need to learn from our existing knowledgebase. While not a necessary condition, I'd wager that it's a likely condition for the AGI system that humanity eventually builds. Leveraging the pre-existing knowledge of thousands of years of progress just seems like too good an opportunity to pass up.

Humanity's knowledgbase, a summary:
* The internet
* Literature
* Art
* Social media
* Interactions (e.g. text)
* Digital environments (i.e. games)
* Experiments / Science

Aspects:
* reasoning <-> knowledge
* depth <-> breadth
* Information comes in many shapes and sizes, some of the most common include:
    * Text: e.g. CommonCrawl is PBs of data [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Crawl)
    * Image, video, datastream
* size


Alternatively, a model could re-learn knowledge for itself, instead gaining all the grounding from direct interaction with the environment.
