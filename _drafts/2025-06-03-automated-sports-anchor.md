Imagine your local game of football that was recorded by your teammate's mother wasn't just plain old footage, but had a live sports anchor calling the shots of every play. Currently, this would require you to make it big, or shell out a big amount of cash, but why not automate it?

With the release of Google's Veo3, it's clear that models have the ability to generate audio with the context of video. Current approaches have been very restricted (e.g. single domain, small datasets), but of course what makes Veo so powerful is the massive dataset and compute that was used to train it.

To generate bigger datasets for custom models, Veo could be used to create new, realistic videos.

Alternatively, the models could leverage existing generative models (i.e. LLMs)
